# Spend Ring

A small offline Android app that reads your Google Wallet payment notifications and shows how much of your budget is left until your next refill.

- No internet permission, no accounts, no APIs. Everything is stored on the phone.
- Ring dashboard: money left, spent, per-day allowance, days to refill.
- Categories, purchase list (tap to edit or delete), past cycles.
- Home-screen widget and an optional "$X left" notification after each purchase.

Every push to `main` builds `SpendRing.apk` and publishes it under Releases.
